Fixed bank changes. Marked (AP) are Archipelago builds only; the rest are in every build.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The fixed bank is $C000-$FFFF.
- Stat build code: $F09E-$F0CC (47 bytes) holds the new code in place of the vanilla penalty code.
- Nintendo header $FFE0-$FFF9 (26 bytes): cleared to $FF. $FFDF stays $80 for the reset code.
- Vertical bridge routine: 80 bytes in the bank 0 map tail if it fits, else in the fixed bank, else five pieces (45, 13, 10, 7, 5) in fixed bank gaps.
- Bank 0 map tail $9D5D-$A652 (search_free_space 0x1d5d-0x2653): whatever the map leaves free. The Responsive Controls read hook (109) and frame hook (59) take it first, then the vertical bridge (80) and the step counter (13), each only when it fits.
- (AP) Run bank and danger meter game-start code: bank 3 when the AP code is there.
- Checksum window (39 bytes, address varies per build): the fixed bank first, then bank 1 spare room ($7F0A up to BANK_1_FREE_END), then, in AP builds only, left out with a build message.
- ROM size: 256 KB, 16 PRG banks of 16 KB. Banks 0-2 and the fixed bank (15, the last) are the original game; 3 is Archipelago code, 4 and 5 music, 6 the music driver and songs, 7 the far-call routines, 8-14 empty ($FF). The game's BRK handler takes the bank from the high nibble of the bank byte (plus 16 if bit 3 is set), and the bank switch at $FF91 writes 5 bits, so every bank up to 15 works with a far call.
- Far call fallbacks (all builds): when the fixed bank has no gap, these go in bank 7 behind a BRK far call (index, bank): Quick Spell name routine (85 in the fixed bank, 83 in bank 7), Encounter Rate game-start code (13), Run Bank and Quick Spell game-start code (20), Responsive Controls frame hook (58) and the read hook (109, also whenever the map tail is too small in an AP build). Quick Spell names and the two game-start routines still leave a 6 byte stub in the fixed bank. The NPC Guillotine routine stays in the fixed bank. The call is `brk, index, $77`: bank 7 in the high nibble, and a low nibble of 7, which the NMI handler at $FF15 needs to tell a far call from other BRKs. The call table is at $8000 with 16 slots (index 0 on), and the routines follow it, up to $BFD7 (the reset stub sits at $BFD8). Bank 6 is all music again. The step counter and the step-end routine stay in the fixed bank or the map tail.
Each of these is checked with find_free_space or dwr_try_free_space. Measured on Gob Standard seeds 2, 7, 10 and 17: music hooks need 112 bytes in pieces of 17 or less.
