DWR TheGobOne Remix 1.3 is out!

Release notes: https://github.com/TheGobOne/dwrandomizer/releases/tag/v1.3

Race checksums changed, so everyone in a race needs 1.3.

__**Gameplay**__
• Warp Whistle adds Hauksness as a 7th stop.
• Return and Warp Whistle land on a nearby grass, desert, hills or trees tile.
• Earth biome bridges and raft docks land on the main island. Mountains that cut off islands are opened to hills.
• NPC Guillotine keeps the NPCs the quest needs.
• Safe Start works with Random Map off.

__**Fixes**__
• Fighter's Ring adds exactly 2. It could add 3.
• Disguised Dragonlord: the hint names the Tantegel throne room, the Tantegel basement and the 2 shrines instead of "a cave".
• Disguised Dragonlord: with *Three's Company* on, Princess Gwaelin is never the Dragonlord.
• Disguised Dragonlord: when Princess Gwaelin is the Dragonlord, the King's new line no longer removes the button wait after it.
• Build Options text: Original builds are -10%/+3 on their stats, not -3/+10%.

__**Site**__
• *Needs* and *Off while* tags show on options, and options grey out when what they need is off.
• Bad flag codes show the error under the Flags box, keep what you typed, and block Randomize until fixed.
• Saved presets have their own column. Deleting one asks in place with Delete or Keep.
• The 1st preset is Full Remix.
• Downloads are named DWR.<seed>.<flags>.nes.

__**Command line**__
• `--race-card on` prints the version, flags, seed and checksum.
• The CLI and the site give the same checksum for the same flags and seed.

Tested in FCEUX and Mesen, not on hardware.

***Any feedback on anything is very welcome!***
